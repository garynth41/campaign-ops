🎛️ Model Parameters

Adjust any assumption and the projection re-runs. The two uncertainty dials matter most: national uncertainty is error that hits every seat together (the kind that makes forecasts wrong), and seat-level uncertainty is independent local variation.

🎯 Seat-by-Seat Projection

Win probability comes from how often each party carried the seat across every simulation. Seats are ordered from safest Labour to safest opposition, so the battleground sits in the middle.

📉 Sensitivity — what would change the result
ℹ️ How this model works

Two inputs, blended per seat. The 2022 result is the starting point (past results are the strongest single predictor of how a seat votes). Your live voter-ID data is blended in, weighted by how much of that seat you have actually identified — a seat with 10% coverage leans mostly on history, a seat with 70% coverage leans on your canvass.

Uncertainty is modelled, not assumed away. Every run simulates the election thousands of times. Crucially, error is split into a national component drawn once per simulation and applied to every seat together, plus an independent local component per seat. Treating seats as independent is the classic modelling error that makes forecasts look far more confident than they should be.

What the outputs mean. A "72% chance of a majority" means Labour won 6+ seats in 72% of simulations — not that it will win 72% of the vote. The tipping-point seat is the one that delivers the majority when seats are ranked by safety; it is where a marginal dollar or hour is worth most.

Limits worth stating. The model has no public opinion polling, so it cannot see late national swings that your canvass has not picked up. It assumes the same parties contest as in 2022. Raise the national uncertainty dial to reflect that.

Record Donation

Donations are recorded against a constituency and are visible only to users assigned to it. All entries, edits, and exports are audit-logged. Keep donor records in line with applicable campaign-finance and data-protection rules.

🔗 Online Payment Links

Card, bank/ACH, and PayPal payments are processed by a payment provider — never inside this app (secret keys and card data must stay server-side for PCI compliance). Paste your hosted payment/donation links below. Supporters click through to pay; confirmed payments come back as donation records.

How to go fully automatic (webhooks)
  1. Create the payment links in Stripe/PayPal (enable recurring for subscriptions).
  2. Deploy a Supabase Edge Function to receive the provider's webhook; keep the secret key there.
  3. On payment succeeded, insert a row into the donations table with the constituency passed as link metadata.
  4. The donation then appears here automatically, marked Received with the transaction ID.
